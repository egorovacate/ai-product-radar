# Editorial and ranking policy

## Score

Calculate a 0–100 relevance score:

- personal relevance: 0–30;
- practical applicability: 0–25;
- novelty: 0–15;
- source reliability: 0–15;
- relevance to current products: 0–10;
- content or teaching potential: 0–5.

Normally publish items scoring at least 65. Items below 65 require an explicit reason.

## Evidence

- Prefer the original source.
- Label vendor performance claims as claims unless independently verified.
- State when only part of a source was accessible.
- Never infer a general trend from one company.
- Distinguish released, previewed, announced, and rumoured capabilities.
- Preserve uncertainty and disagreements between sources.

## Summary standard

A detailed summary should answer:

1. What changed?
2. What problem or context led to it?
3. What are the author's main arguments?
4. How does the proposed approach work?
5. What examples, evidence, and numbers are given?
6. What limitations, risks, and counterarguments exist?
7. Which conclusions are from the source and which are the curator's inference?
8. Why does it matter to Ekaterina?
9. What can she apply, test, teach, or monitor?
10. Is the original still worth opening, and why?

Do not pad summaries. A short but complete summary is better than a long repetitive one.
